# LSRI Amplify — LSRI (Life Science Research Infrastructures) Amplify

LSRI Amplify will strengthen and integrate Europe's Life Science Research Infrastructures (LSRIs) by creating a user-centred, AI-enabled access ecosystem that makes it easier for researchers, innovators, policymakers and industry to discover, access and combine services across the Health and Food ESFRI domain. Bringing together all 16 ESFRI Life Science Research Infrastructures, the project addresses the fragmentation of access to Europe's world-class facilities, data resources and expertise. It pursues three objectives: (1) deliver an integrated European access ecosystem based on interoperable service catalogues, harmonised access procedures and AI-assisted, challenge-driven navigation; (2) enhance coordination, interoperability and operational alignment across LSRIs through shared standards, common frameworks, cross-infrastructure pilots and knowledge exchange; and (3) establish a coherent, visible and future-ready LSRI ecosystem contributing to European policy priorities. The consortium is coordinated by EMBL; DKFZ contributes as an affiliated entity of BBMRI-ERIC.
