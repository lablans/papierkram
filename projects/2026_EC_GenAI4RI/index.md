# GenAI4RI — A Generative AI Framework for Access, Orchestration and Validation across European Life Sciences Research Infrastructures

GenAI4RI proposes a next-generation, generative-AI digitalisation framework for European life-sciences research infrastructures (RIs). Its core is a shared, open, MCP-enabled Service Layer that lets RI data and services be prepared, exposed, discovered and combined across infrastructures such as BBMRI-ERIC, ECRIN-ERIC, EATRIS-ERIC and the European Genome-phenome Archive (EGA). On top of it, agentic Virtual Assistants serve two audiences: an RI Operator VA that automates labour-intensive operations (FAIRification, metadata deduplication, cross-RI resource linking, regulatory knowledge-base maintenance), and an Industrial Access VA that gives SMEs, startups and corporate R&D teams a single natural-language gateway to discover datasets, navigate clinical-investigation and AI-Act requirements, and prepare regulatory documentation — turning fragmented RIs into a coherent innovation ecosystem. Coordinated by Universitat de Barcelona across a 15-partner consortium, the framework is validated through cross-domain pilot use cases. DKFZ participates as the German BBMRI National Node, contributing the BBMRI Sample Locator and Bridgehead and their MCP integration for federated, Negotiator-mediated discovery of and access to biosamples and associated data.
