# COHESION — Cross-domain One-Health Environment for Sovereign, Interoperable and Optimised iNference

COHESION aims to design, build, and validate Europe's first sovereign, federated, and green AI inference infrastructure for One Health research. It addresses four structural failures in cross-domain biomedical research — unknown data fitness before access, no infrastructure for cross-domain AI training or inference, lack of European sovereign reference databases, and undocumented AI energy consumption — by deploying a containerised COHESION Node at each partner institution. These nodes host agentic AI that traverses a Distributed Knowledge Graph across European research infrastructures (BBMRI-ERIC, SHARE-ERIC, MIRRI-ERIC, and others), enabling federated model training and inference across human, environmental, climatic, and microbial data spaces, while ensuring no raw data ever leaves its home institution. DKFZ contributes to federated learning, privacy-preserving analytics (DataSHIELD/Armadillo), and One Health use case demonstrators including a cancer–exposome linkage study leveraging BBMRI-ERIC biobank collections.
